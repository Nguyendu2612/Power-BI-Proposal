Shopper Receipt Tracking & Behavior Analytics

Power BIDAXPower QueryMobile app data designPeriodic reporting

A continuously running consumer panel. Panelists scan and upload their receipts through a mobile app, each receipt is parsed and cleaned in an internal web tool, and the line-level data (SKU, date and time, amount, quantity, store) feeds ongoing analysis of brands, baskets and shopper journeys.

My role

  • Designed the data capture flow for the app: which fields to record and at what level of detail, so the data arrives analysis-ready and matches client requirements.
  • Built the dashboard that turns the incoming receipt stream into trend, ranking and behavior views.
  • Ran ad-hoc analyses and custom metrics when the standard views could not answer a client's question.
  • Wrote periodic reports that turn dashboard numbers into a story for stakeholders.

Analysis covered

  • Trends: purchase volume and spend over time, by brand and category
  • Market basket: which SKUs and brands are bought together
  • Brand & SKU ranking: share of wallet, penetration, repeat rate
  • Shopper journey: purchase frequency, store preference, switching over time
Built to support the panel

Two internal tools that keep the data clean

The client dashboard is only as good as the receipts behind it. These two tools are used by the operations team every day.

Panelist Upload Streak

A focused Power BI tool that checks which panelists are active and eligible, so only qualified panelists feed the main analysis. It went through three versions as the team added requirements, and now also calculates rewards.

  • > 10 receipts in 30 days
  • > 7 days in the panel
  • rejection rate < 20%
  • not on the blocked list

My role

  • Gathered requirements with the operations team in detail before building.
  • Sourced data through Power BI dataflows from the team's database.
  • Cleaned and shaped it in Power Query (blank phone numbers, a calendar table, MonthYear and WeekNum sorting).
  • Wrote the criteria in DAX, choosing row context or measures depending on the result needed.

Output

A monthly list of qualified panelists for any chosen period, which keeps the main project's panel clean and eligible, and a reward view for panelists who meet the validity rules.

Data Quality Check

A guided dashboard for non-technical team members to review and clean incoming receipt data section by section before it reaches the client dashboard. It confirms every section was updated each day and flags cleaning steps still outstanding.

My role

  • Mapped the data sections that need daily cleaning and wrote a checklist for each, based on the most common problems in raw uploads (wrong SKU mapping, cheating, inappropriate uploads).
  • Designed a simple guided interface so cleaners work correctly without touching queries or DAX.
  • Built daily freshness checks that flag any section falling behind.
  • Logged a status for every cleaner action and used that log to calculate each cleaner's pay for the work they actually completed.

Output

A daily checklist of cleaning status for every data section, so only clean, complete data reaches the official dashboard.

Competitor Analysis Dashboard

Audit researchPower QueryDAXDrill-through

The client wanted to compare two leading pharmacy chains, Pharmacity and Long Châu. We ran audit research at store level, and I turned it into a dashboard that estimates each chain's revenue and foot traffic. I proposed the dashboard layout, and the client approved it: direct, actionable metrics for analysts in an efficient layout.

  • ~100receipts cleaned over 2 weeks
  • 2chains compared weekly and monthly
  • 3outputs: revenue, traffic, market metrics

My role

  • Client alignment: clarified requirements and KPIs, agreeing to estimate revenue and traffic each month.
  • Data cleaning: cleaned about 100 receipts over two weeks, reviewed store video footage to count foot traffic, and reformatted the data in Power Query.
  • Model and dashboard: built the data model and relationships, wrote DAX measures, and created drill-through pages in Power BI.

Output

  • Estimated weekly and monthly revenue per chain
  • Estimated weekly and monthly foot traffic
  • Market metrics comparing the two chains' store performance and positioning

Retail Basket Analytics

AI OCR receiptsVBASQLPower QueryDAX

Shoppers at a Lotte supermarket submitted receipts through our in-house app, which reads them with AI OCR. I mapped the products, built the model and delivered a dashboard on shopping frequency, basket size and product mix.

  • ~550SKUs matched to master data
  • 2 daysto map them, using VBA fuzzy matching
  • 1SQL source feeding Power BI

My role

  • Data cleaning: used VBA approximate matching to map about 550 SKUs to master data within two days, created master records for new items, and integrated them into the team's SQL database.
  • Dashboard: queried SQL as the single source for Power BI, did one-time shaping in Power Query, wrote DAX measures, built the visuals and pulled out the insights.

Delivered

  • Customer demographics
  • Incidence rate by product, category and brand
  • Average SKUs and categories per transaction
  • Average order value
  • Cross-selling: how often two products appear in one basket
